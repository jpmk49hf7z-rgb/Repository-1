## SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of `[DATE]` (the "Effective Date") by and between `[YOUR LEGAL ENTITY NAME]`, a `[STATE]` limited liability company ("Provider"), and `[CLIENT LEGAL ENTITY NAME]`, a `[STATE]` `[ENTITY TYPE]` ("Client").

**1. Services.** Provider will research, write, design, and build web pages for Client as described in Exhibit A (the "Services" and, individually, "Deliverables"). Exhibit A is incorporated into this Agreement. Anything not described in Exhibit A is outside the scope of this Agreement.

**2. Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of `[60 DAYS / 3 MONTHS]` (the "Initial Term"), then renews month-to-month until terminated under Section 11.

**3. Fees and Payment.** Client will pay Provider `[$AMOUNT]` per month, plus a one-time onboarding fee of `[$AMOUNT]` due on signing. Monthly fees are invoiced in advance on the `[FIRST]` day of each month and are due within `[7]` days. Fees are non-refundable except as provided in Section 5. Invoices more than `[15]` days past due accrue interest at `[1.5%]` per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower, and Provider may suspend Services until the balance is paid.

**4. Approval and Publication.** Provider will submit each Deliverable to Client for review. **No Deliverable will be published to any live Client property without Client's approval.** Client is responsible for reviewing each Deliverable before approving it, including verifying the accuracy of all statements about Client's own products, pricing, and customers. Client's approval, or Client's publication of a Deliverable, constitutes Client's acceptance of it. Where Client has asked Provider to publish on Client's behalf, Provider will publish only Deliverables Client has approved in writing.

**5. Delivery Guarantee.** If Provider fails to deliver the first `[SIX (6)]` Deliverables within thirty (30) days after Client has provided the source materials described in Section 6, Client's next monthly fee is waived. This is Client's sole remedy for that delay. **Provider makes no representation, warranty, or guarantee regarding search rankings, traffic, leads, pipeline, conversion rates, or revenue.** Search engine results depend on factors outside Provider's control, including third-party algorithms, Client's market, and Client's product.

**6. Client Responsibilities.** Client will: (a) provide the source materials reasonably necessary for the Services, including product documentation, current pricing, positioning, competitive information, and — where available — sales call recordings or transcripts; (b) designate one primary contact authorized to approve Deliverables; (c) review and approve or reject Deliverables within `[5]` business days; and (d) respond to Provider's requests for information within `[3]` business days. Timelines in Exhibit A, including the guarantee in Section 5, extend day-for-day for any delay caused by Client. Provider is not responsible for the accuracy of information Client supplies.

**7. Changes to Scope.** Neither Party is bound by a change to the Services until both Parties agree in writing, including any change to fees or timelines. Email confirmation from each Party's authorized representative satisfies this requirement.

**8. Comparative and Third-Party Content.** Deliverables may reference third parties, including Client's competitors. Provider will base such references on information that is publicly available or supplied by Client at the time of writing, and will represent third parties accurately and fairly. Provider may decline to produce content it reasonably believes is inaccurate, misleading, disparaging, or infringing, and doing so is not a breach of this Agreement. Client is responsible for confirming, before approving any Deliverable, that its statements about third parties are accurate and consistent with Client's legal obligations. Third-party information can change after publication; Provider is not responsible for maintaining the accuracy of published references except through refresh services expressly included in Exhibit A.

**9. Production Methods and Authorship.** Provider uses artificial intelligence tools, along with human research, editing, and review, to produce Deliverables. Every Deliverable is reviewed by a human before delivery. Client acknowledges and accepts this. **If Client is subject to any policy, contract, or regulation restricting AI-assisted content, Client must disclose it in writing before the Effective Date.** Client will not attribute any Deliverable to a named individual author who has not reviewed and approved it. Provider makes no representation as to the copyrightability of AI-assisted material under any jurisdiction's law.

**10. Intellectual Property.** On Provider's receipt of full payment for the period in which a Deliverable was created, Provider assigns to Client all right, title, and interest it holds in that Deliverable. Provider retains all right, title, and interest in its pre-existing and independently developed materials, including templates, page frameworks, research methods, prompts, and know-how, and nothing here prevents Provider from serving other clients in Client's industry or from reusing its general knowledge and experience. Client grants Provider a non-exclusive license to use materials Client supplies solely to perform the Services.

**11. Confidentiality and Data.** Each Party will keep the other's non-public information confidential, use it only in connection with this Agreement, and protect it with at least the care it uses for its own confidential information. This does not apply to information that is public through no fault of the receiving Party, was rightfully known before disclosure, or must be disclosed by law with prompt notice where permitted. This Section survives termination for `[3]` years. Client will not supply Provider with personal data beyond what the Services require, and is responsible for having a lawful basis for any personal data it supplies — including obtaining any consent required before sharing recordings or transcripts of calls with third parties. Provider will not sell or share Client's confidential information and will delete or return it within `[30]` days of written request following termination.

**12. Limitation of Liability.** To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither Party is liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary, or punitive damages, or for lost profits, revenue, or data, even if advised of the possibility. Each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement will not exceed the total fees paid by Client to Provider in the `[THREE (3)]` months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim. These limits do not apply to breaches of Section 11, to indemnification obligations under Section 13, or to liability that cannot be limited by law.

**13. Indemnification.** Each Party will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the other from third-party claims arising out of the indemnifying Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct, or violation of law in connection with this Agreement. Client will additionally indemnify Provider against third-party claims arising out of (a) information Client supplied, (b) any Deliverable as published after Client's approval, including claims of false advertising, defamation, or unfair competition brought by a third party referenced in it, and (c) Client's products, services, or other marketing.

**14. Independent Contractor.** Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing here creates an employment, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship. Provider controls the manner and means of performing the Services and is responsible for its own personnel, subcontractors, tools, taxes, and insurance, and remains responsible for subcontractors' compliance with Section 11.

**15. Publicity and Case Studies.** Provider may identify Client as a client and use Client's name and logo in its marketing. Provider may publish a case study describing the Services and their results **only with Client's prior written approval of the specific content**, which Client may withhold. Client may revoke this permission on written notice, after which Provider will remove the material from channels it controls within `[30]` days.

**16. Termination.** After the Initial Term, either Party may terminate for any reason on `[30]` days' written notice. Either Party may terminate immediately if the other materially breaches and fails to cure within `[15]` days of written notice. On termination Client will pay for all Services performed through the effective date, and Provider will deliver all Deliverables completed as of that date, in editable form, within `[15]` days. Sections 10, 11, 12, 13, and 17 survive.

**17. General.** This Agreement, including Exhibit A, is the entire agreement between the Parties on this subject and supersedes all prior discussions and proposals. It may be amended only in a writing signed by both Parties. It is governed by the laws of the State of `[STATE]` without regard to conflict-of-laws rules, and the Parties consent to exclusive jurisdiction and venue in the state and federal courts in `[COUNTY, STATE]`. Neither Party may assign without the other's written consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of substantially all assets. If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder stands and that provision is modified to the minimum extent needed to make it enforceable. Notices are effective when sent to the email addresses below. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.

## EXHIBIT A — STATEMENT OF WORK

**Service tier:** `[LAUNCH / GROWTH / SCALE]` — `[$AMOUNT]`/month
**Initial term:** `[60 DAYS / 3 MONTHS]` beginning `[DATE]`

**Monthly deliverables**
- `[NUMBER]` web pages researched, written, designed, and built per month, drawn from: competitor comparison pages, alternatives pages, integration pages, use-case pages by industry or role, migration pages, and supporting landing pages
- On-page SEO, schema markup, and internal linking for each page
- `[QUARTERLY / MONTHLY]` refresh of pages previously built by Provider
- `[QUARTERLY / MONTHLY]` competitive gap analysis
- Monthly report: pages shipped, indexation status, and available performance data

**Onboarding (first 30 days)**

| Days | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | Kickoff, source material handoff, voice and style reference built |
| 6–10 | Competitive gap analysis and page map, ranked by buyer intent |
| 11–14 | Page templates designed and approved |
| 15–30 | First `[SIX (6)]` pages delivered for approval |

**Publication method:** `[PROVIDER SUBMITS PULL REQUEST / PROVIDER PUBLISHES APPROVED PAGES IN CLIENT CMS / CLIENT PUBLISHES]`

**Review turnaround:** Client approves or returns each Deliverable within `[5]` business days. Provider's business hours are `[9:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m. TIMEZONE]`, Monday–Friday, excluding US federal holidays.

**Source materials Client will provide at kickoff:** product documentation, current pricing, positioning and ICP, competitor list with known win/loss reasoning, and `[2–3]` sales call recordings or transcripts. Existing customer quotes, case studies, and objection notes if available.

**Out of scope.** Management of Client's CMS, hosting, or existing content; standing administrative access to Client's systems; paid media; top-of-funnel or general blog content outside the page types above; email marketing; social media; brand identity or logo design; custom software development; same-day turnaround; monitoring or on-call response; and any claim about Client's product that Client's source materials do not support.
