# Week 1 Deliverable 2 — Service Agreement Template

**Status:** Draft template, pending one-time legal review | **Milestone:** "Niche and offer defined (Day 7)"

> **Read this before using the template.** This is a starting draft, not legal advice, and it has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Before you send it to a paying client, have an attorney licensed in your state review it once — this is a one-time cost of a few hundred dollars that covers you for every client afterward. Pay particular attention to the limitation-of-liability and indemnification sections, which is where state law varies most and where a template found online is most likely to be unenforceable.
>
> Fill every `[BRACKETED]` field. An agreement sent out with brackets still in it costs you the deal.

## SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of `[DATE]` (the "Effective Date") by and between `[YOUR LEGAL ENTITY NAME]`, a `[STATE]` limited liability company ("Provider"), and `[CLIENT LEGAL ENTITY NAME]`, a `[STATE]` `[ENTITY TYPE]` ("Client"). Provider and Client are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

**1. Services.** Provider will perform the operations-automation services described in Exhibit A (the "Services"). Exhibit A is incorporated into this Agreement. Services not described in Exhibit A are outside the scope of this Agreement.

**2. Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of `[60 DAYS / 3 MONTHS]` (the "Initial Term"), after which it renews automatically on a month-to-month basis until terminated under Section 11.

**3. Fees and Payment.** Client will pay Provider `[$AMOUNT]` per month, plus a one-time onboarding fee of `[$AMOUNT]` due on signing. Monthly fees are invoiced in advance on the `[FIRST]` day of each month and are due within `[7]` days of the invoice date. Fees are non-refundable except as stated in Section 4. Invoices unpaid more than `[15]` days past due accrue interest at `[1.5%]` per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law, whichever is lower, and Provider may suspend Services until the balance is paid. Client is responsible for the cost of any third-party software licenses or subscriptions required to deliver the Services, which are billed directly to Client by those vendors unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

**4. Delivery Guarantee.** If Provider fails to deliver the first `[TWO]` automated workflows described in Exhibit A within thirty (30) days after Client has provided all system access described in Section 6, Client's next monthly fee is waived. This is Client's sole remedy for that delay. Provider makes no representation, warranty, or guarantee regarding Client's revenue, sales, conversion rates, cost savings, or other business results.

**5. Changes to Scope.** Either Party may request a change to the Services. No change is binding until both Parties agree to it in writing, including any change to fees or timelines. Email confirmation from an authorized representative of each Party satisfies this requirement.

**6. Client Responsibilities.** Client will: (a) provide Provider with the system access, credentials, and third-party account permissions reasonably necessary to perform the Services, using dedicated service accounts rather than an individual employee's personal login wherever the platform supports it; (b) designate one primary point of contact authorized to approve deliverables and scope changes; and (c) respond to Provider's requests for information, approvals, or access within `[3]` business days. Provider is not responsible for delays caused by Client's failure to meet these responsibilities, and timelines in Exhibit A extend day-for-day for any such delay.

**7. Intellectual Property.** Upon Provider's receipt of full payment for the period in which a deliverable was created, Client owns the specific automated workflows, configurations, and documentation Provider builds for Client within Client's own accounts and systems. Provider retains all right, title, and interest in its own pre-existing materials, templates, tooling, methodologies, and know-how, including anything developed independently of this Agreement, and nothing in this Agreement prevents Provider from providing similar services to other clients or from reusing its general knowledge and experience.

**8. Confidentiality.** Each Party will keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other Party in connection with this Agreement, will use it only to perform or receive the Services, and will protect it with at least the same care it uses for its own confidential information. This obligation does not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was rightfully known to the receiving Party without a duty of confidentiality before disclosure, or is required to be disclosed by law — in which case the receiving Party will give the other Party prompt notice where legally permitted. This Section survives termination for `[3]` years.

**9. Data Protection.** Each Party will comply with applicable data protection laws in connection with the Services. Provider will not access, use, or retain Client's customer personal data except as reasonably necessary to perform the Services, will not sell or share it, and will delete or return it within `[30]` days of termination at Client's written request. Client is responsible for having a lawful basis for any customer data it makes available to Provider and for its own privacy notices and consents. If the Services require Provider to process personal data subject to the GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or a comparable law, the Parties will execute a data processing addendum before that processing begins.

**10. Limitation of Liability.** To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither Party is liable to the other for indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary, or punitive damages, or for lost profits, lost revenue, or lost or corrupted data, even if advised of the possibility. Each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement will not exceed the total fees paid by Client to Provider in the `[THREE (3)]` months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim. These limitations do not apply to a Party's breach of Section 8, to a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 12, or to liability that cannot be limited under applicable law.

**11. Termination.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement after the Initial Term for any reason on `[30]` days' written notice. Either Party may terminate immediately on written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within `[15]` days of written notice describing it. On termination, Client will pay for all Services performed through the effective date of termination, and Provider will, within `[15]` days, deliver documentation for all workflows built for Client and transfer or revoke its access to Client's systems as Client directs. Sections 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, and 15 survive termination.

**12. Indemnification.** Each Party will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the other Party from third-party claims arising out of the indemnifying Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct, or violation of applicable law in connection with this Agreement. Client will additionally indemnify Provider against third-party claims arising out of Client's products, marketing content, or customer data, or out of Provider's compliance with Client's written instructions.

**13. Independent Contractor.** Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship. Provider controls the manner and means of performing the Services and is responsible for its own personnel, subcontractors, taxes, and insurance. Provider may engage subcontractors to perform the Services and remains responsible for their performance and for their compliance with Sections 8 and 9.

**14. Publicity and Case Studies.** Provider may identify Client as a client and use Client's name and logo in Provider's marketing materials. Provider may publish a case study describing the Services and their results **only with Client's prior written approval of the specific content**, which Client may withhold. Client may revoke this permission at any time on written notice, after which Provider will remove the material from channels it controls within `[30]` days. Neither Party will disclose the other's confidential information in any publicity without prior written consent.

**15. General.** This Agreement, including Exhibit A, is the entire agreement between the Parties on this subject and supersedes all prior discussions and proposals. It may be amended only in a writing signed by both Parties. It is governed by the laws of the State of `[STATE]`, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules, and the Parties consent to exclusive jurisdiction and venue in the state and federal courts located in `[COUNTY, STATE]`. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of substantially all assets. If any provision is held unenforceable, the rest remains in effect and the unenforceable provision is modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable. Notices must be in writing and are effective when sent to the email addresses below. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.

## EXHIBIT A — STATEMENT OF WORK

**Service tier:** `[STARTER / GROWTH / SCALE]` — `[$AMOUNT]`/month
**Initial term:** `[60 DAYS / 3 MONTHS]`, beginning `[DATE]`

**Monthly deliverables**
- `[NUMBER]` new automated workflow(s) built, tested, and deployed per month
- Monitoring and repair of all workflows built by Provider under this Agreement
- Monthly performance report: hours saved, tickets deflected, errors caught, against the baseline recorded in the onboarding audit
- `[CADENCE]` check-in call

**Response times**
- Broken workflow affecting orders or customer communication: `[SAME / NEXT]` business day
- All other requests: `[2]` business days
- Business hours are `[9:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m. TIMEZONE]`, Monday through Friday, excluding US federal holidays.

**Onboarding schedule (first 30 days)**

| Days | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | Kickoff call, access provisioning, tool inventory |
| 4–7 | Operations audit and written automation roadmap |
| 8–14 | Automation #1 built, tested, deployed |
| 15–21 | Automation #2 built, tested, deployed |
| 22–30 | First monthly performance report |

**Supported platforms.** Services cover the following systems only: Shopify, `[HELPDESK]`, `[EMAIL/SMS PLATFORM]`, `[FULFILLMENT/3PL]`, Slack, and Google Workspace. Work on any other system requires a written scope change under Section 5.

**Out of scope.** Custom software development; ongoing manual data entry or ticket handling; paid media planning, buying, or management; store platform migration; creative, copywriting, or design work; and any work on systems not listed above.

**Baseline measurement.** During the onboarding audit the Parties will record the current manual effort for each process targeted for automation. That recorded baseline is the reference point for all hours-saved figures in Provider's monthly reports.
