## The two numbers that matter

**Kill criteria:** fewer than 2 paying retainers and under $8,000 cumulative
revenue at the **end of month 4** → stop selling and reassess. That is in the
plan on purpose so the decision gets made rather than drifted past.

**Base case:** ~$387,000 revenue in year one, ~$70,900 exit MRR, roughly 4
hours a week of your time.

## The one thing to verify early

The plan rests on a published figure that **51% of B2B tech brands have zero
citations** in AI answers. That is also the one number we can measure ourselves
for almost nothing — the scanner does it directly. Week 2 runs it across 1,200
companies. If the real number is much lower, the addressable market shrinks and
the offer needs rethinking. See `RESEARCH-NOTES.md` §8.
