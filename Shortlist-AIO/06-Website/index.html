Why it happens

Answer engines recommend vendors they can corroborate across the open web. Brand mentions correlate with AI citations roughly three times more strongly than backlinks do — and about 75% of what drives a citation sits on domains you do not own. Traditional SEO was built to win a link on your own page. That is now the smaller half of the problem.

What we will not promise

We do not control ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, or Google. Nobody does. Any firm guaranteeing you a citation rate is either misunderstanding the systems or misrepresenting them.

We commit to defined work at a defined volume, and we show you the measurement either way. Movement typically takes 60 to 90 days to appear, and we would rather say that now than explain it in month two.

How we measure

The method is published because it should be checkable. If a number in our report is wrong, we want you to be able to tell.

Mentions and citations are not the same thing

Engines routinely name a vendor without linking to it. A mention puts you on the shortlist; a citation sends you traffic. We report them separately, because the gap between them tells you which problem you actually have — and collapsing them into one "visibility score" would hide exactly the thing worth knowing.

Failed calls are never counted as absence

If an engine times out, that answer is excluded from every denominator rather than scored as a miss. An API failure is not evidence that nobody recommends you, and reporting it as one would make your position look worse than it is.

Ambiguous brand names get stricter matching

If your brand is also an ordinary English word — Notion, Monday, Ramp, Front — naive text matching finds you everywhere and inflates your numbers. We require corroboration before counting a match, so "on Monday we ship" does not become a citation.

Official APIs only

Every measurement runs through the providers' official paid APIs. We do not scrape answer engines in breach of their terms, and we would not want your reporting resting on something that stops working the week they tighten it.

Our ethical floor

We do not write, buy, or broker reviews. We do not post as you, run unattributed accounts, or build sites that pose as independent comparison pages. Where a tactic requires a human voice, it is a named person at your company, posting as themselves, with their affiliation disclosed.

Some of that is the FTC's rule on reviews and testimonials, which carries penalties near $52,000 per violation and reaches conduct a company "should have known" about. The rest is that the shortcut version of this work is the reason the category will eventually get regulated.
