## Trademark note — read before filing the DBA

**Filing the DBA will not itself cause an infringement problem.** A Manitoba
business name registration is a provincial filing; it does not create
trademark rights and does not adjudicate anyone else's.

But it also gives you **no protection**, and there are existing users of the
name. See `03-Legal-For-Review/README.md` for the detail and the specific
question to put to your reviewer.
